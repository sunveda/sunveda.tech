# Health App and Familiar website identities

Original, hand-authored SVG artwork for the in-development announcements. No external artwork, fonts, scripts or linked resources are embedded.

- `health-app-icon-v1.svg`: a cream heart within a mint leaf on deep teal, suggesting health and wellbeing without an official medical or identity-provider emblem.
- `familiar-icon-v1.svg`: an adult and child inside a rounded video frame, with a small golden play symbol on plum, suggesting family learning and reviewed video.

Each is a 256 × 256 vector icon used consistently in homepage cards and the app catalogue. Product names are adjacent text; repeated decorative image instances use empty alt text. The artwork is a website identity, not evidence of an app-store release or an integration. Versioned filenames and `?v=1` follow the existing asset cache convention.
