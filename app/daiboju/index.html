Privacy by design · プライバシー

We keep only

  • Where to send the alert, encrypted
  • Your language
  • Your prefecture

We never keep

  • Your name or an account
  • Your location. The route is found on your phone
  • Anything about you in the AI. It writes one message per area and language

Every message has a link that deletes your data in one tap. Alerts come from official Japan Meteorological Agency data. Always follow instructions from your city, police and fire services.

Built by SunVeda with Gemini 4 Argon and Google Maps. Someone collapsed? Find an AED with AEDoko.

* The first alert is sent in English for these languages; the next steps are written in your language.
